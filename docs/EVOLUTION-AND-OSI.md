# From curation to governed evidence and action

Informative explanation, September 15, 2026. Published baseline when written:
v0.81. Current edition: GKOS-2026-09-24 v0.82.1, with the same normative
population. Development context: accepted R22 (informative) and R23
(prospective). No qualification claim.

GKOS separates evidence, assertions, controls, review, context and authorized use.
It does not preserve absolute truth or mandate an operating system, event bus,
single Governing Agent, database or canonical-writer implementation.

OKF+ is historical terminology. The current exchange contract is GKX 2.0;
implementations such as Engine and Kosmos-Oden realize bounded capabilities.
Source preservation remains subject to applicable governed retention and erasure.
A protected write boundary enforces authorized effects; it need not be one agent.

## Authority and replay

Deterministic mandatory restrictions cannot be replaced by model confidence.
L5 review may be performed by an authorized human or a separately authorized
independent Review Agent within the adopted conditions. This is bounded
disposition authority, not unrestricted self-approval. Major or indeterminate
cases follow required escalation; lack of the required approver means HOLD.

Capture operative selection before reproducible context assembly when required.
Validate authority again at action time. Record attempts, refusals and outcomes;
an outcome returning as evidence receives no inherited approval.
