# Standards engagement and assurance boundaries

Informative strategy, September 15, 2026. GKOS v0.81 is owner-authorized,
developmental, non-consensus and non-qualifying. This document establishes
neither an organization nor a certification scheme.

## Separate objectives

| Objective | Required distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Consensus specification | Published due process, balanced participation and dispositions |
| Developer accreditation / ANS approval | Accreditation of procedures differs from approval of a document |
| Product, process or service certification | Scheme scope and competent impartial certification; consider ISO/IEC 17065 |
| Personnel certification | Separate scheme; consider ISO/IEC 17024 |
| Management-system certification | Separate scheme; consider ISO/IEC 17021-1 and applicable requirements |
| Legal opinion | Qualified counsel, jurisdiction, facts, assumptions and date |
| Regulatory authorization | Particular product/function and applicable government process |

Framework references are scoping pointers, not accreditation claims or a verified
clause-level mapping. Confirm current editions and applicability with the
selected body before designing a scheme.

## Practical sequence

Continue bounded pilots, conformance evidence and independent implementation
work alongside early community engagement. Do not make v1.0, a new legal entity
or a completed healthcare profile a universal prerequisite for exploratory contact.

W3C Community Groups offer incubation, public discussion and test development.
Their reports are not W3C standards or endorsement. Transition is separate and
not guaranteed; consult the [W3C process](https://www.w3.org/community/about/process/)
and [standards-track readiness guidance](https://www.w3.org/guide/standards-track/).

For ANSI routes, review the selected developer's procedures and the
[Essential Requirements](https://ansi.org/american-national-standards/ans-introduction/essential-requirements).
Treat entity structure, patent policy, funding and marks as owner decisions;
do not transfer ownership or create a body through an editorial update.

Use the [current roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) for v1.0 governance and evidence gates.
Different ownership is desirable but not mandatory for an independent
implementation under the [September 12 clarification](decisions/2026-09-12-implementation-independence.md).
That does not remove the separate independence requirements for claims of
independent verification.

NIST comments and informative crosswalks are contributions, not recognition.
Certification, consensus approval and regulatory acceptance remain separate
from signed publication, archive identity and successful implementation tests.
