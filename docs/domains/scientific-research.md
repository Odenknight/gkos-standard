# Scientific research

Informative pilot guide, September 15, 2026; GKOS v0.81 baseline.
Not an adopted sector profile or assurance claim.

Proposed first question: Public dataset-to-claim traceability. Identify the exact source revision,
assertion, context selection, controls, disposition and permitted use.

Negative and boundary tests should cover retraction, contradictory evidence and irreproducible context.
Record expected and observed results, unsupported cases and correction routes.
Freeze implementation, policy, fixture and environment coordinates before testing.

Requirement mapping is pending pilot scoping: select and review exact applicable
IDs from the [registry](../../requirements/REGISTRY.md); do not invent sector IDs
or infer whole-profile coverage. External obligations require separate dated
primary-source review and competent sector review before consequential use.

See the [domain evidence rules](README.md). No deployment, certification,
regulatory acceptance or independent effectiveness is established here.
