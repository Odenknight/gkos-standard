assertion, context selection, controls, disposition and permitted use.

Negative and boundary tests should cover out-of-scope effects, expired authority and changed policy.
Record expected and observed results, unsupported cases and correction routes.
Freeze implementation, policy, fixture and environment coordinates before testing.

IDs from the [registry](../../requirements/REGISTRY.md); do not invent sector IDs
or infer whole-profile coverage. External obligations require separate dated
primary-source review and competent sector review before consequential use.
