# Informative graphics

The graphics in this directory are informative and non-normative. They help
readers orient themselves to GKOS; they do not amend the master standard or an
accepted development decision.

No graphic establishes certification, compliance, endorsement,
implementation, or conformance. The master standard and accepted development
decisions control whenever a graphic and authoritative text differ.

The current edition is GKOS-2026-09-24 v0.82.1, a documentation patch of the
developmental specification (public working draft). Its technical baseline is
v0.82, and its normative population is unchanged from v0.81.

- The [graphics register](REGISTER.md) lists every image and diagram, with its
  source, use, edition label and status. It also lists the graphics still
  needed (`GN-001` and up).
- Current architecture, refund, adoption, workflow and CIA diagrams, with SVG,
  PNG and editable sources, are documented in [`diagrams/`](diagrams/README.md).
- The illustrated figures are documented in
  [`../illustrated/`](../illustrated/README.md).
- Repository-sharing artwork is documented in [`social/`](social/README.md).
- Legacy orientation graphics remain at this directory level. They are
  historical v0.75 and v0.76 artwork, except the provisional logo. They are
  described in [`../archive/graphics/`](../archive/graphics/README.md).
